Pare Dental for Linux (64-bit Intel/AMD or ARM)

1. Unpack this folder somewhere it can stay, for example ~/Applications/Pare Dental.
2. Run ./paredental  (double-click it, or start it from a terminal).
3. Optional: run ./paredental --install once to add Pare Dental to your applications menu.

Pare Dental opens in its own window when Google Chrome, Chromium, Edge, Brave or Vivaldi is
installed; otherwise in your default browser.

Your designs are kept in ~/.paredental/designs and backups in ~/Documents/Pare Dental Backups.
Pare Dental updates itself when a new build is out.

Pare Dental is based on layerling (https://github.com/henmedia/layerling), AGPL-3.0-only.
